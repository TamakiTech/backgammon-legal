Privacy Policy

Backgammon by Tamaki is a board game. It does not create accounts, show ads, or sell information about you.

Play on this device stays on your phone or computer. The names you type and the sound setting are stored only on that device so the next sitting can remember them. A random seat id is kept for that browser session so a hosted table can tell the seats apart.

Online rooms are optional. When you host or join, the name you type, the room code, and the moves in that room go to the Backgammon room server so the other player can play with you. That data is kept only while the table is open. We do not ask for an email, phone number, or payment card. Traffic to the room server uses HTTPS.

Invite links include a short room code. Anyone with the link can try to join that sitting, so share them with people you mean to play with.

The Android app needs internet permission so those rooms can connect. Local play and play against Tamaki still work if you never host or join.

Backgammon does not use an advertising ID. You can delete local data by clearing the app’s storage.

Backgammon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Questions: TamakiTech on GitHub. This policy is for Backgammon 1.0.
